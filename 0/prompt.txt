  ## Deviations from the brief (and why)
  1. **Added an `AdoptedExact` event** (Pending→AlreadySatisfied, **Recovery only**) alongside `ObservedExactBeforeEffect` (Pending→AlreadySatisfied, any kind). Without it the `run kind` parameter would be vacuous, and the recovery-only privilege of adopting a pre-existing effect into a missing journal would be unenforced. Preflight uses the former, `reconstruct_missing_state` the latter.
  2. **`RecoveredExact` now yields `Published`, not `AlreadySatisfied`** (brief said Attempting→Published; this changes observable receipt outcomes for an interrupted-then-confirmed operation). This is the audit's #13 fix: "our attempt landed" is no longer conflated with "pre-existing".
  3. **`dispatch_registry` now always re-observes and requires `Exact` before success**, for every ecosystem, instead of trusting `PublishOutcome::Published`. Consequence: a **PyPI** publish now fails at dispatch (`RegistryPublishUnconfirmed`) rather than later at receipt construction — it already failed end-to-end (audit P1-4b), just later. Cost: one extra registry query after the confirmation poll for cargo/npm.
  4. **Collapsed sibling APIs** (audit #14): `execute_release{,_with_artifacts,_with_artifacts_in_recovery}` → one `execute_release(capability, store, permit, envelope, artifacts)`; `observe_release_operations{,_with_artifacts}` → one. `reconcile_release_execution` now takes `Option<&state>` so the read-only `release reconcile` path needs no envelope.
  5. **Fixed a pre-existing schemars defect in scope**: container-level `#[schemars(with = "…Wire")]` is ignored by the derive, so `ReleaseReceiptV1`/`ReleaseExecutionStateV1` were publishing their in-memory shape (snake_case, maps) instead of the wire shape. Replaced with hand-written `JsonSchema` impls delegating to the wire structs — otherwise the new schema guard would have pinned a wrong schema.
  6. **No `docs/` or `docs/specs/` updates.** `docs/07-self-release-lifecycle.md` and `SPEC-SELF-RELEASE-LIFECYCLE.json` now describe the old provenance-at-receipt-time model and the bare four-state observation. Out of scope for S1 per the brief, but they are stale and should be a follow-up.
  7. **New E-codes**: `E177` (observation unusable as evidence — role mismatch, internal defect), `E178` (run envelope invalid for this intent). `RemoteConflict::RegistryVersionDiffers` added.
