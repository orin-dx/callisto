- No change was needed in `crates/callisto-moon/src/locator.rs`: since `MoonProjectLocator` holds one long-lived `IdentityResolver` across both `projects()` and `declared_edges()`, memoizing at the resolver level automatically eliminates both reported redundancies (re-resolving `from` per edge, and re-resolving `to` once per edge instead of once per unique target).
- Evidence: `identity_resolve_count_test.rs` shows 30 `resolve()` calls for the same `(path, ecosystem)` → 1 disk read. `locator_identity_read_count_test.rs` builds a shared `utils` package with 5 dependents and shows `projects()` + `declared_edges()` together do 6 reads total (one per manifest), not 6 + 5 (edges) = 11.
