## The finding that matters most

The spec's line numbers are accurate — I checked every one, and the earlier canon-verifier/canon-auditor corrections did land. But the spec diagnoses bug 1 in the wrong function.

`solve_cascade` already contains a **Linked**-group convergence block at `crates/callisto-graph/src/cascade.rs:314-379`: it iterates `input.groups.linked.values()`, computes one shared `winner` version, assigns it to every member, and sets `changed = true` so the outer `while changed` loop re-converges. **There is no Fixed-group counterpart.** That missing block is the bug.
