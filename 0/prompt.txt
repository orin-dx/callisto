### callisto-format

- `CargoToml::update_dependency_spec()`'s inherited-dependency delegation to `WorkspaceCargoResolver`, including the self-referential "this manifest IS the workspace root" invariant guard (293-312) — untested.
- `WorkspaceInheritance::inherited()` itself (505-511) — never called by any test.
- `WorkspaceCargoResolver::load()`'s relative-path branch and read/parse failures (534-550), `workspace_version()`'s both branches (592-609), `write_version()` (612-644) and `write_dependency()` (646-708) — essentially the entire write side of workspace-level dependency/version editing is untested.

This is a coherent, high-priority finding, not scattered noise: whoever wrote the inherited-version detection logic (`open()`, line 47-52) clearly designed for this feature, but no test exercises what happens when a member Cargo.toml actually uses it.
