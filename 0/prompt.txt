## Summary

**Bug:** `[[linked-group]]` and `[[fixed-group]]` entries in `callisto.toml` were silently ignored. `GroupTable::validate_syntactic()` was called on the raw groups, but `GroupTable::resolve()` was never called. `ResolvedConfig.groups` was always set to `GroupTable::default()`.

**Fix (3 files changed):**

1. **`crates/callisto-graph/src/config/resolve.rs`** — Added `pub(crate) raw_groups: RawGroupTable` field to `ResolvedConfig` and moved `raw_groups` into the struct at the end of `load()` (instead of discarding it after validation).

2. **`crates/callisto-graph/src/lib.rs`** — In `Workspace::load`, changed `config` binding from `let` to `let mut` and added one line after `ManifestWalkResolver::build()`:
   ```rust
   config.groups = GroupTable::resolve(&amp;config.raw_groups, graph.identity())?;
   ```

3. **`crates/callisto-graph/src/config/groups.rs`** — Removed `#[allow(dead_code)]` from `GroupTable::resolve` since it is now called.
